AI Usage Buddy

AI Usage Buddy is a macOS app that shows the remaining Codex 5-hour and weekly allowance included in your ChatGPT plan, using a character in the menu bar. Click it to see the remaining percentage and the time until reset.

You connect an account through the official OpenAI sign-in. A "sample data" view is also available so you can try the screens without connecting an account (it does not show your actual usage).
