AI Usage Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Operator

The macOS app "AI Usage Buddy" (the "App") is provided by PUREROAST&BREW, LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY (the "Operator"). This policy describes the information the App handles.

Data collection by the Operator

The Operator does not collect or receive your information through the App. The Operator does not run its own servers for the App, and the App does not include advertising, in-app purchases, or its own analytics.

However, as described below, the OpenAI Codex app-server bundled with the App (the "bundled helper") communicates with OpenAI's servers. That communication is handled by OpenAI, not by the Operator.

Information stored on your Mac

The following is stored on your Mac, in the App's sandbox container (under Application Support).

  • Settings: the selected account, which allowance to display, notification settings, language, refresh interval, launch at login, shortcuts, and similar.
  • Account information: an account identifier, a display name, and the email address and plan type obtained through the official integration (when available).
  • Allowance snapshots: remaining 5-hour and weekly allowance, reset times, fetch time and status, manual adjustment values, and records of notifications already shown.

The bundled helper manages sign-in credentials. The App launches the bundled helper with a setting that specifies storage in the macOS Keychain, but the Operator has not directly verified how credentials are actually stored in the Keychain. The App itself does not read your passwords or authentication tokens.

No fixed retention period is defined. Stored information remains on your Mac until you delete it as described below or delete the App.

Communication with OpenAI

When you start connecting an account, the bundled helper performs the official OpenAI sign-in, fetches account information, and fetches your allowance. During sign-in, an authentication URL opens in your default browser, which communicates with OpenAI's site.

The bundled helper may also communicate with OpenAI's servers for other purposes. The Operator has not been able to determine the full content of this communication, or OpenAI's retention periods and purposes. For how OpenAI handles information, please see the OpenAI Privacy Policy.

The App starts the bundled helper with settings that disable analytics and telemetry, but this does not guarantee that no other communication takes place.

Sample data view

The "sample data" view lets you try the screens without connecting an account. It only displays fixed fictional values; it does not communicate over the network, start the bundled helper, sign in, save data, or send real notifications. The numbers shown are not your actual usage. Quitting or restarting returns the App to its normal state.

macOS notifications and launch at login

If you enable notifications and grant macOS permission, the App asks macOS to show a notification when the allowance runs low, containing the account display name, the remaining amount, and the threshold. Launch at login is registered only if you explicitly enable it; when enabled, the App starts after you sign in to macOS and tries to refresh your allowance.

Deleting an account

When you delete an account connection in the App, the App asks the bundled helper for an official sign-out and, after confirming success and that the helper has stopped, tries to delete the managed home folder, the account entry, allowance data, adjustment values, and related settings on your Mac. If a step such as sign-out fails, local deletion is not started, your information is kept, and an error is shown.

Please note:

  • This does not delete your OpenAI account itself.
  • It does not guarantee that the authentication on OpenAI's side is revoked.
  • If file deletion or saving fails, only part of the information may be deleted.
  • Working and temporary folders outside the managed home folder are not covered by this deletion.
  • We cannot guarantee that the sandbox container is automatically removed when the App is uninstalled.

Update checks and the support link

The App Store version of the App does not communicate to check for updates. The support link in Settings opens GitHub Issues in your external browser only when you click it.

This page and the support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you view them, GitHub records and stores your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you are signed in; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is GitHub's processing when you view a web page and is separate from how the App operates. What you post to GitHub Issues may be public. Please do not post personal information or credentials.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children, and the Operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the effective date. For significant changes, we will make this clear on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the App, please use GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information or credentials.
