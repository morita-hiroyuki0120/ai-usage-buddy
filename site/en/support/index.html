FAQ

Why do I need to sign in?

The remaining Codex 5-hour and weekly allowance is tied to your own ChatGPT account. The App connects your account through the official OpenAI sign-in, then fetches and displays the allowance. Without signing in, the allowance cannot be fetched.

Can I look at the screens without signing in?

While no account is connected, use "Try with sample data" in the first-run setup or in Settings under Accounts to view the screens with fictional sample data. The sample data view does not communicate over the network, sign in, save data, or send real notifications, and the numbers are not your actual usage. Quitting or restarting returns the App to its normal state.

What happens when I delete an account?

When you delete an account connection in Settings, the App asks for an official sign-out and then tries to delete the account entry and stored allowance data on your Mac. Your OpenAI account itself is not deleted, and revocation of the authentication on OpenAI's side is not guaranteed. See the Privacy Policy for details.
